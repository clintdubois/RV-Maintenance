# Rear Suspension Upgrade Reference — Hellwig Sway Bar + Agile Fox Shocks

Reference notes on a rear suspension upgrade (Hellwig sway bar + Agile-tuned Fox shocks) reported by another owner in a similar coach — captured here for evaluating whether it's worth pursuing on this 27R.

## Owner Report (source)

Posted to the [View/Navion Motorhomes Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/ViewNavionMotorhomes/permalink/10163309569298359/):

> 2027-24R. With 4500 MB chassis and 4 cyl engine. After two trips, we decided to "upgrade" the rear suspension to improve ride and sway. Installed Hellwig 7777 sway bar and Agile 2.5 Dia Fox rear shocks. (Fyi: we had this done to our 2022-24D). After picking up the unit after the installation, both my wife and I noticed the improvement and are happy so far. Next trip is Sunday to make sure. An independent RV shop did the installation and only charged my 2 hrs of labor.

**Key details:**
- Coach: 2027 Winnebago View, 24R floorplan — same generation/floorplan as our 27R
- Chassis: Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 4500, 4-cylinder engine
- Parts: Hellwig 7777 rear sway bar + Agile 2.5" diameter Fox rear shocks
- Labor: ~2 hours, done by an independent RV shop (not a dealer)
- Feedback so far: both owners noticed an improvement in ride quality and sway after the first drive; a further trip was planned to confirm
- This same owner had already done the identical upgrade on a previous coach (2022 View 24D) — this is a repeat purchase from someone with direct before/after experience on two different Views

## ⚠️ Open Question: 4500 Chassis Compatibility

The specific Agile "RV/Class C" Fox 2.5 kit found above is explicitly listed as fitting **Sprinter 3500 only** — the product page states it does **not** cover the 4500. This doesn't match the Facebook poster's own stated chassis (4500), so one of the following is likely:

- Agile sells a separate, differently-listed SKU for the 4500 that didn't surface in this search, or
- The 3500 and 4500 share enough rear-axle/leaf-spring architecture that the same shock physically fits both even though Agile's product page doesn't say so, or
- The independent shop sourced a different/compatible shock rather than this exact listed kit.

**Before ordering:** confirm 4500 fitment directly with Agile Off-Road (or the shop that did this install, if we can identify them) rather than assuming the listed 3500 kit is a drop-in fit.
