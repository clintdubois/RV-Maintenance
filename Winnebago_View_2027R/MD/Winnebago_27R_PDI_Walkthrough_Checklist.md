# Winnebago View 27R — Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI) & Walkthrough Checklist

Purpose: a working checklist for delivery day so nothing gets missed. Pairs with [Winnebago_27R_Operations_Guide.md](Winnebago_27R_Operations_Guide.md) (systems reference) and [Winnebago_27R_New_Owner_Orientation.md](Winnebago_27R_New_Owner_Orientation.md) (questions to ask/have demoed). This doc stays action-only — look up the "why" there.

**Ground rule (iRV2, Alliance RV Owners, Jayco Owners forums):** don't sign final paperwork or release payment until everything below is satisfactory — you lose most leverage once you sign. Bring your own copy of this checklist rather than relying on the dealer's PDI form; theirs reportedly covers far fewer points (~100) than a thorough owner-driven walkthrough.

## Before delivery day

- [ ] Confirm appointment length — block 2–3+ hours minimum, a half-day if you can; don't let it get rushed
- [ ] Ask Poulsbo to de-winterize / have all systems live (water, power, propane) before you arrive
- [ ] Ask Poulsbo to power on the fridge/freezer **the day before** — it needs a full 24 hours to reach temperature
- [ ] Confirm who's doing the walkthrough — request the factory-trained tech, not sales
- [ ] Bring:
  - Phone + charging cable (Winnebago Connect pairing, USB port test)
  - Notebook/this checklist, camera
  - Small plug-in device (night light/charger) to spot-test 110V outlets
  - GFCI tester, if you have one
  - Instant-read thermometer (hot water and fridge/freezer temps)
  - Small induction-compatible pot or pan (to actually confirm the cooktop heats)
  - Bubble level (to independently verify the auto-leveling system)

## Paperwork to collect

- [ ] Final bill of sale matching the negotiated out-the-door number
- [ ] Title/registration paperwork
- [ ] Factory warranty documents — coach and Mercedes chassis (separate warranties; see [Extended Warranty](#extended-warranty) below for the service contract paperwork)
- [ ] Owner's manuals: coach, Mercedes Sprinter chassis, appliances (Truma, Lithionics, generator, MBUX)
- [ ] Build sheet / window sticker for this VIN, itemizing factory options
- [ ] **Open recalls**: ask whether any chassis recalls apply to this VIN and are completed — e.g. RCRIT-20V468, a 2020–2021 Sprinter seat-wiring-harness recall
- [ ] **Federal certification / weight sticker** (driver-side doorjamb) — photograph it. This is the authoritative GVWR/GAWR/UVW for *your* unit, not a spec-sheet figure
- [ ] Keys: ignition, entry door, storage bays, gas cap — confirm count, get spares cut if only one set
- [ ] Winnebago Connect account setup / login credentials

## Exterior walkthrough

- [ ] **Baseline photos/video first, before touching anything else** — every exterior panel, the roof if accessible, every interior room/cabinet. This is your dated "before" record for any later dispute (damage claim, punch-list disagreement, insurance).
- [ ] Full walk-around for panel damage, scratches, or delamination
- [ ] Tires: DOT date codes, pressure, matching brand/size, spare present and correct pressure, tread checked for embedded nails/debris
- [ ] Exterior lights: running, brake, turn, backup
- [ ] Slide-out seals: intact, no gaps or tears
- [ ] Awning: full extend/retract, fabric condition, LED lighting
- [ ] Gear garage: both doors open/close/latch, L-track intact, light and 110V/12V ports work
- [ ] Exterior shower: hot and cold
- [ ] City water inlet, fresh fill, dump valves: cap correctly, dust caps present
- [ ] Propane tank: level and connections
- [ ] Entry door: alignment, latches without forcing — have the tech show the "push near the handle" technique (see [New Owner Orientation → Entry door & general handling](Winnebago_27R_New_Owner_Orientation.md#entry-door--general-handling))

## Undercarriage & roof

The two areas easiest to skip on a normal walk-around.

- [ ] Roof: seams/sealant checked for bubbles or lifting if safely visible; press up on interior ceiling panels/vents for soft spots (water intrusion sign)
- [ ] Frame rails, crossmembers, body mounts: no rust-through, cracks, or loose brackets
- [ ] Leveling jack brackets and hydraulic lines: securely bolted, no leaks or weeping
- [ ] Tank mounting straps: level and secure — recheck once tanks are filled (see Kitchen and Water system below)
- [ ] Exhaust, chassis and generator: hangers intact, no rattling, scraping, or soot staining
- [ ] Suspension: leaf springs/shocks intact, hardware present
- [ ] Wiring harnesses and hoses: clipped/secured, not resting on anything hot or sharp
- [ ] Driveshaft and U-joints: no obvious play or excessive wear
- [ ] Brake lines/hoses: no damage, cracking, or leaks
- [ ] Propane line routing: secure, no kinks or damage
- [ ] DEF tank and lines (separate from the diesel tank): secure, no damage or leaks

## Chassis / dash walkthrough

- [ ] Odometer matches paperwork
- [ ] Engine fluids topped off: oil, coolant, power steering, brake, washer fluid
- [ ] Dash warning lights: none illuminated at startup
- [ ] DEF level and top-off point confirmed (see [Operations Guide → Chassis & driving](Winnebago_27R_Operations_Guide.md#chassis--driving-mercedes-sprinter) for tank capacity/jug guidance)
- [ ] MBUX paired to your phone, WiFi hotspot tested
- [ ] Fuel level reasonable for the drive home
- [ ] Mirrors, digital rearview mirror, backup camera: test at multiple speeds, not just Reverse (see [New Owner Orientation](Winnebago_27R_New_Owner_Orientation.md))

## Interior systems

Grouped by system below — operate everything live, don't just glance at it.

### Power & electrical

- [ ] Winnebago Connect touchscreen: cycle lighting, climate, water levels, battery status — for climate, actually switch on heat and confirm the propane furnace fires, not just that the touchscreen shows a heat icon
- [ ] All 110V/USB outlets live, GFCI outlets trip/reset test
- [ ] Breaker panel: labels match actual breakers (trip one to confirm)

#### Shore power

- [ ] Battery charges from shore power — confirm rate/status changes
- [ ] Air conditioner: cooling and heat pump (heating) modes both work

#### Generator

- [ ] Start it, run a few minutes, confirm it carries AC/high-draw loads; note the hour meter (should read near zero)
- [ ] Battery charges from the generator, separately from shore power
- [ ] Air conditioner: repeat cooling/heat pump test on generator power only, to confirm it can start and carry the load

#### House battery

- [ ] State of charge display reasonable
- [ ] Solar charging indicator shows input (if sunny)
- [ ] Inverter: run a 120V load off battery only, generator/shore off

### Kitchen and Water system (Andrea)

- [ ] Water heater: hot water in ECO and Comfort modes — verify the actual temperature difference with your thermometer
- [ ] Fill the fresh tank, run every faucet (including outside shower) — check under sinks, behind panels, and at fittings for leaks while flowing
- [ ] Water pump: cycles on, builds pressure, shuts off cleanly — no continuous running or chatter (purge air at every faucet first if the unit was just de-winterized)
- [ ] Toilet, black/gray tank level sensors correct; dump valves — no leaks while draining
- [ ] Fridge/freezer actually cold — verify with your thermometer (~35–38°F fridge, ~0–5°F freezer)
- [ ] Induction cooktop and LP burner both ignite — confirm heat with your own induction-compatible pot
- [ ] Microwave/convection oven: powers on, runs a test cycle
- [ ] Kitchen USB-A/USB-C outlets charge a real device, not just show power
- [ ] Every drawer/cabinet: open fully, check for shipping debris or shifted/damaged contents
- [ ] Windows and blinds/shades: every window opens/closes, screens intact, shades cycle

### Leveling, slide-out & bed

- [ ] Leveling jacks: full auto-level cycle, verify level with your own bubble level (not just the touchscreen chime), then test manual override each corner
- [ ] Slide-out: full electric cycle, smooth operation, trim doesn't bind; have the tech show the manual/hand-crank override
- [ ] Flex Bed: convert twin → combined and back, with the tech demonstrating the mechanism hands-on

### Safety & accessories

- [ ] Smoke, CO, and LP gas detectors: test buttons, confirm not expired
- [ ] Included accessories present: sewer hose + support/ramp, fresh water hose, shore power cord, wheel chocks, leveling blocks, entry step, microwave accessories (turntable, screens, manual), tool kit or spare fuses

## Confirm against build sheet

- [ ] **Generator**: confirmed 3,200W propane, not diesel — brand still unconfirmed, worth a glance at the nameplate (see [Operations Guide → Power system](Winnebago_27R_Operations_Guide.md#power-system-house-batteries-solar-inverter-generator))
- [ ] **Leveling jack brand**: factory Equalizer/EQ Systems vs. any HWH upgrade
- [ ] **GVWR/GAWR/UVW** off the certification sticker, not a marketing page — Winnebago's own published figures for this floorplan have been inconsistent across sources, so the sticker is authoritative
- [ ] **Rearview camera**: reverse-only or live while driving, and at what speed cutoff (forums report ~15 mph)

## Wrap-up

- [ ] Photo/video the tech's demonstrations (leveling cycle, slide operation, Flex Bed conversion) if allowed — separate from the baseline condition photos taken at the start
- [ ] Ask what maintenance is due in year one — chassis service intervals, filters, seals/seams recheck, anything time- or mileage-based
- [ ] Plan to get the loaded coach weighed at a CAT scale, per-axle, before any towing — real tow margin is GCWR (15,250 lb) minus actual loaded weight, not a curb-weight estimate
- [ ] Confirm the dealer's punch-list process in writing, with a timeline

## Extended Warranty

- [ ] Extended service contract documents in hand, if purchased
- [ ] **Contract administrator name in writing** — the entity that actually pays claims, not just the marketing brand (e.g. "Winnebago XtraRide" is a brand; the administrator behind it, likely Protective Asset Protection, is what matters)
- [ ] Term purchased matches what was negotiated — see [Negotiation Playbook → Extended warranty negotiation](Winnebago_27R_Negotiation_Playbook.md#extended-warranty-negotiation) for the pricing benchmarks
- [ ] Coverage split confirmed: coach warranty vs. Mercedes chassis warranty vs. this contract, and who to call for each
- [ ] Exclusions confirmed — diesel/Sprinter contracts vary on whether chassis electronics, turbo, and emissions systems are covered
